Brainrot Base Rush — a 3D Steal-a-Brainrot-style game: buy absurd brainrot characters off a conveyor, place them on your base, earn cash every second, steal from rivals, lock your base, outrun the Brainrot Storm, and rebirth for a permanent income multiplier. Twelve original characters on six rarity rungs (Common to Secret) plus Golden, Diamond and Rainbow mutations. Built with Rojo + Luau, no uploaded images or sounds.

## How to play

Walk to the conveyor and press E (or click) to buy a brainrot, then guard your base with L. Steal from rivals, slap thieves carrying your brainrots, survive the storm, and rebirth to multiply all income.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
